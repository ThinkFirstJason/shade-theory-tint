After you drive away

Film is installed wet. The water has to work its way out through the film, and the sun does most of that for you here.

Day 0–3

Keep the windows rolled up. The bottom edge is still setting and can peel if it catches the door seal.

Day 3–7

Small hazy patches or water bubbles are normal. Leave them alone. They'll bake out in the sun.

Day 7+

Clean with soap and water or an ammonia-free cleaner and a microfibre towel. No blades.

Day 30

Still seeing a bubble? Come back. We'll fix it free, no appointment needed.
